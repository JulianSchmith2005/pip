PipA pocket sprout that lives in a toy and on your screen

Plays here on its own. Connect the toy over USB to let it take over.

Pip is pottering about.

How to play

Tap a pad
Pat. Pip bounces and the lights pulse yellow.
Hold a pad
Stroke. Pip calms down with a slow blue wave.
Hold both
Hug. Warm pink glow and a big boost to your bond.
Three quick taps
Tickle. Great for mood, tiring for Pip.
Leave Pip be
Pip gets lonely, then sleepy, then falls asleep.

Timers run fast so you can see every state in a minute or two: Pip gets lonely after 40 seconds alone. The toy runs the same rules and light patterns, so the screen and the toy always agree.
